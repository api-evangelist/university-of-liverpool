The University of Liverpool has achieved its highest-ever overall positivity score in this year’s National Student Survey (NSS), with more than 85% of our students reporting a positive experience in the 2026 survey. This outstanding achievement reflects growing satisfaction in students’ experiences at Liverpool, with improvements recorded in six of the survey’s seven themes.
