The University has secured ten Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Postdoctoral Fellowships to boost our research excellence. The MSCA Fellowships provides support for researchers seeking to enhance their careers through international mobility, training and interdisciplinary collaboration. They also enable fellows from outside the UK to undertake research at the University.
