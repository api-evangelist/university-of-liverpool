Scientists at the Universities of Liverpool and Oxford have developed a promising new way to kill antibiotic-resistant strains of Neisseria gonorrhoeae, the bacterium that causes gonorrhoea, using a targeted therapy that is activated at the bacterial surface while minimising damage to human cells.
