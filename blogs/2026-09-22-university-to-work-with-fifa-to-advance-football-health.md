The University of Liverpool and FIFA have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) establishing a four-year collaboration aimed at strengthening methodological research practices across global football. The partnership brings together FIFA Medical’s commitment to improving player health and wellbeing worldwide with the University of Liverpool’s expertise in outcome measurement and reporting in clinical research.
